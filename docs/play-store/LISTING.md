## Full description (max 4000 characters)
SENTRA 360 Security connects you directly with your private security company.

REPORT INCIDENTS
• Report crime, suspicious activity, accidents, fires, medical emergencies and road hazards
• Add photos, short videos, documents and your GPS location
• Get a reference number and follow every step: received, officer assigned, on scene, resolved

PANIC BUTTON (for clients with an active protection service)
• Hold the button for 3 seconds to alert the control room (protection against accidental presses)
• Sends your GPS location (when you allow it) and keeps updating it while the alert is open
• Choose a silent alert, add a note, and optionally notify your emergency contacts
• See clearly when your alert was received, seen by the control room, acknowledged and when an officer was dispatched

PROTECT YOUR HOME AND VEHICLE
• Register your properties and vehicles
• Apply for armed response, alarm monitoring, patrols, CCTV, electric fencing, access control and vehicle protection
• Receive quotations in the app, accept them and pay

BOOK SECURITY SERVICES
• VIP and personal escorts, event security, security assessments, installations and more
• Choose the date, time, place and duration and get a confirmed booking

STAY INFORMED
• Community safety alerts for your area
• Notifications for every update on your reports, applications and bookings

YOUR PRIVACY
Your address, location, vehicle details and emergency contacts are only visible to authorised staff of your security company, and access is logged. Designed with South Africa's POPIA in mind. You can delete your account at any time.

IMPORTANT
This app does not replace the emergency services. Alerts need an internet connection and cannot be sent while your phone is offline. In a life-threatening emergency, also call SAPS on 10111 or an ambulance on 10177.
